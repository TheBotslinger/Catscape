# 📃 NOTES!
- This modification is all client-sided and does not give the player any advantages that others cannot get. It's all just reskins of an enemy made to become a battle cat unit/enemy!
- This modification is only for PC Only! (unless fleasion is on mobile I guess.)

# What is Catscape?
 Catscape is a Fleasion config where every enemy is customized to have it's own unique Battle Cats enemy that represents or is similar to the Nullscape enemy! Catscape is a small project with a combination of Nullscape and Battle Cats!

# Fleasion Download!
If you do not have Fleasion, [download it from here,](https://github.com/fleasion/Fleasion/releases) and look for a tutorial on how to use Fleasion!

# How to use!


Once you have learned the gist of how to insert configs, go and Download the new JSON file in [placeholder] & place it in your configs folder. Once it's in, Open your configs and enable your JSON! Once you have enabled it, you should clear your cache, and then open Roblox, and you're done!
